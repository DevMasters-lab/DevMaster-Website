05 Offers and placements | expert_offers | EXPERT_OFFER | Core ER entity | 12 | One DevMasters-to-expert offer, distinct from final selection. | Create offer and version 1 in one transaction. request/expert/candidate/request_role identity is immutable after first issue. 05 Offers and placements | offer_versions | OFFER_VERSION | Core ER entity | 26 | Versioned terms and operational state of one expert offer. | UNIQUE(offer_id, version_no). Only drafts can change terms or attachment membership. After issue, only lifecycle metadata can change. Starting a revision supersedes the old version and invalidates it for new responses; past responses remain. | S1 §§2–4
05 Offers and placements | offer_responses | OFFER_RESPONSE | Core ER entity | 10 | Immutable expert response to an exact issued offer version. | Many comments but at most one decision response per version. A new decision after changes/decline requires a new version. All acceptance channels satisfy current-version/expiry checks at recording; issued_at <= responded_at <= recorded_at. Late evidence is a comment, not backdated valid acceptance. | S1 §§2–4
05 Offers and placements | selection_decisions | SELECTION_DECISION | Core ER entity | 10 | Append-only DevMasters selection decisions, separate from acceptance. | All decisions follow valid acceptance, matching the accepted workflow. Allow at most one SELECTED event per offer. A later NOT_SELECTED correction must cancel the existing placement atomically, not delete it. A new placement cycle requires a new offer. | S1 §§2–4
05 Offers and placements | placements | PLACEMENT | Core ER entity | 17 | Administrative record of a selected expert working directly with the company. | No tasks, timesheets, deliverables or project milestones. Negotiated material changes require a fresh accepted offer cycle; do not silently rewrite the historic terms link. | S1 §§2–4
